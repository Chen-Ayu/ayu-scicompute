## PySCF

PySCF is installed separately and retains its Apache-2.0 license. Cite PySCF and any functional/dispersion libraries used in scientific work.

## ORCA

ORCA is not bundled and must not be described as open source. Users obtain it from the official source and determine whether academic or commercial terms apply.

## Materials Studio

Materials Studio is not bundled. Users supply an authorized installation, Gateway, and license environment. The adapter must not activate, unlock, patch, redistribute, or bypass it.

## Gaussian and VASP roadmap

Gaussian and VASP are planned interoperability targets, not bundled dependencies and not implemented adapters in this release. Users must obtain lawful software access. VASP POTCAR files and related licensed pseudopotential data must never be committed or redistributed by this repository.
