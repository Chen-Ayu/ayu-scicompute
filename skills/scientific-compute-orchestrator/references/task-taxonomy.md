# Task taxonomy and minimum scientific contract

## Finite molecules and clusters

Record composition, coordinates/connectivity, protonation, charge, multiplicity, conformer origin, solvent model, method, basis, dispersion and requested observables. Transition-metal systems additionally require candidate oxidation/spin states and a multireference-risk note.

## Periodic materials and metals

Record crystal structure and provenance, periodic dimensionality, composition/occupancy, magnetic order candidates, pseudopotential family, exchange-correlation functional, dispersion or Hubbard U when used, plane-wave cutoff, k-point density, smearing, electronic/ionic thresholds and cell-relaxation policy.

## Surfaces, adsorption and defects

Record slab orientation/thickness, vacuum, fixed layers, supercell, defect charge/corrections, adsorption sites, reference energies, dipole correction and finite-size convergence.

## Polymers, gels and molecular dynamics

Record repeat-unit chemistry, chain length/distribution, tacticity/crosslinking, composition, initial density, force field, charge method, packing replicas, ensemble sequence, thermostat/barostat, timestep, equilibration evidence, production length and uncertainty/replicas.

## Comparative claims

Keep structures, methods, basis/pseudopotentials, boundary conditions, solvent, standard states and numerical settings consistent. A single converged calculation is not a trend, mechanism or validated predictive model.
